## Cited, not included

Lessons cite textbooks (for example NSCA's *Essentials of Strength Training and
Conditioning* and *Essentials of Sport Science*), journal articles, and public
documentation such as MLB Statcast glossary pages. Those works are not
included. Hub text is newly written and was checked for copied passages.
Joint range-of-motion reference values are factual numbers cited to their
published sources.

## Removed from this edition

The author's private study edition also used a club's internal manual,
licensed course materials, and job-application prompts. None of that material,
and no lesson that names it, is in this repository.
